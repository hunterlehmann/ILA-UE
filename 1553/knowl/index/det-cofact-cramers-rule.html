Theorem 4.2.16 Cramer’s Rule.

Let \(x = (x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n)\) be the solution of \(Ax=b\text{,}\) where \(A\) is an invertible \(n\times n\) matrix and \(b\) is a vector in \(\R^n\text{.}\) Let \(A_i\) be the matrix obtained from \(A\) by replacing the \(i\)th column by \(b\text{.}\) Then
\begin{gather*} x_i = \frac{\det(A_i)}{\det(A)}\text{.} \end{gather*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
First suppose that \(A\) is the identity matrix, so that \(x = b\text{.}\) Then the matrix \(A_i\) looks like this:
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \amp b_1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp b_2 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp b_3 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp b_4 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Expanding cofactors along the \(i\)th row, we see that \(\det(A_i)=b_i\text{,}\) so in this case,
\begin{gather*} x_i = b_i = \det(A_i) = \frac{\det(A_i)}{\det(A)}\text{.} \end{gather*}
🔗
Now let \(A\) be a general \(n\times n\) matrix. One way to solve \(Ax=b\) is to row reduce the augmented matrix \((\,A\mid b\,)\text{;}\) the result is \((\,I_n\mid x\,).\) By the case we handled above, it is enough to check that the quantity \(\det(A_i)/\det(A)\) does not change when we do a row operation to \((\,A\mid b\,)\text{,}\) since \(\det(A_i)/\det(A) = x_i\) when \(A = I_n\text{.}\)
  1. Doing a row replacement on \((\,A\mid b\,)\) does the same row replacement on \(A\) and on \(A_i\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \amp b_2 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right) \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = R_2-2R_3}\;\\ \amp\left(\begin{array}{lll|l} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ a_{21}-2a_{31} \amp a_{22}-2a_{32} \amp a_{23}-2a_{33} \amp b_2-2b_3 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right)\\ \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = R_2-2R_3}\; \left(\begin{array}{lll} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21}-2a_{31} \amp a_{22}-2a_{32} \amp a_{23}-2a_{33} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{array}\right)\\ \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp b_2 \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = R_2-2R_3}\; \left(\begin{array}{lll} a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21}-2a_{31} \amp b_2-2b_3 \amp a_{23}-2a_{33} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{array}\right)\text{.} \end{align*}
    In particular, \(\det(A)\) and \(\det(A_i)\) are unchanged, so \(\det(A)/\det(A_i)\) is unchanged.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Scaling a row of \((\,A\mid b\,)\) by a factor of \(c\) scales the same row of \(A\) and of \(A_i\) by the same factor:
    \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \amp b_2 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right) \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = cR_2}\; \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ ca_{21} \amp ca_{22} \amp ca_{23} \amp cb_2 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right)\\ \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = cR_2}\; \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ ca_{21} \amp ca_{22} \amp ca_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix}\\ \quad\begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp b_2 \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_2 = cR_2}\; \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ ca_{21} \amp cb_2 \amp ca_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
    In particular, \(\det(A)\) and \(\det(A_i)\) are both scaled by a factor of \(c\text{,}\) so \(\det(A_i)/\det(A)\) is unchanged.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Swapping two rows of \((\,A\mid b\,)\) swaps the same rows of \(A\) and of \(A_i\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \amp b_2 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right) \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_1\longleftrightarrow R_2}\; \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \amp b_2 \\ a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \amp b_1 \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \amp b_3 \end{array}\right)\\ \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_1\longleftrightarrow R_2}\; \begin{pmatrix} a_{21} \amp a_{22} \amp a_{23} \\ a_{11} \amp a_{12} \amp a_{13} \\ a_{31} \amp a_{32} \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix}\\ \quad\begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ a_{21} \amp b_2 \amp a_{23} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix} \amp\;\xrightarrow{R_1\longleftrightarrow R_2}\; \begin{pmatrix} a_{21} \amp b_2 \amp a_{23} \\ a_{11} \amp b_1 \amp a_{13} \\ a_{31} \amp b_3 \amp a_{33} \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
    In particular, \(\det(A)\) and \(\det(A_i)\) are both negated, so \(\det(A_i)/\det(A)\) is unchanged.
    🔗
