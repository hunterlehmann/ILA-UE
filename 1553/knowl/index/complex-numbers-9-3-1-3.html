Item.

Complex conjugation replaces \(i\) with \(-i\text{,}\) and is denoted with a bar:
\begin{gather*} \bar{a+bi} = a - bi\text{.} \end{gather*}
The number \(\bar{a+bi}\) is called the complex conjugate of \(a+bi\text{.}\) One checks that for any two complex numbers \(z,w\text{,}\) we have
\begin{gather*} \bar{z+w} = \bar z + \bar w \sptxt{and} \bar{zw} = \bar z\cdot\bar w\text{.} \end{gather*}
Also, \((a+bi)(a-bi) = a^2 + b^2\text{,}\) so \(z\bar z\) is a nonnegative real number for any complex number \(z\text{.}\)
