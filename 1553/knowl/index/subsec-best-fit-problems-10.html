Example 6.4.21 Best-fit trigonometric function.

What is the best-fit function of the form
\begin{gather*} y=B+C\cos(x)+D\sin(x)+E\cos(2x)+F\sin(2x)+G\cos(3x)+H\sin(3x) \end{gather*}
passing through the points
\begin{gather*} \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ -1.5 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ .5 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -.5 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix},\; \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}? \end{gather*}
\def\labeledpt(#1)#2{\point["{$(#1)$}" #2] at (#1)} \begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1.35, thin border nodes, whitebg nodes] \draw[grid lines] (-5,-2) grid (5,2); \draw[->] (-5,0) -- (5,0); \draw[->] (0,-2) -- (0,2); \labeledpt(-4,-1){{below}}; \labeledpt(-3,0){{below}}; \labeledpt(-2,-1.5){below}; \labeledpt(-1,.5){above}; \labeledpt(0,1){{above}}; \labeledpt(1,-1){below}; \labeledpt(2,-.5){below}; \labeledpt(3,2){below}; \labeledpt(4,-1){above}; \end{tikzpicture}
🔗

Solution.

We want to solve the system of equations \small\[ \spalignsysdelims.. \left\{\begin{alignedat}{2} -1 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(-4) + D\sin(-4) + E\cos(-8) + F\sin(-8) + G\cos(-12) + H\sin(-12) \amp {} \\ 0 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(-3) + D\sin(-3) + E\cos(-6) + F\sin(-6) + G\cos(-9) + H\sin(-9) \amp {} \\ -1.5 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(-2) + D\sin(-2) + E\cos(-4) + F\sin(-4) + G\cos(-6) + H\sin(-6) \amp {} \\ 0.5 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(-1) + D\sin(-1) + E\cos(-2) + F\sin(-2) + G\cos(-3) + H\sin(-3) \amp {} \\ 1 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(0) + D\sin(0) + E\cos(0) + F\sin(0) + G\cos(0) + H\sin(0) \amp {} \\ -1 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(1) + D\sin(1) + E\cos(2) + F\sin(2) + G\cos(3) + H\sin(3) \amp {} \\ -0.5 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(2) + D\sin(2) + E\cos(4) + F\sin(4) + G\cos(6) + H\sin(6) \amp {} \\ 2 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(3) + D\sin(3) + E\cos(6) + F\sin(6) + G\cos(9) + H\sin(9) \amp {} \\ -1 \amp {}={} \amp B + C\cos(4) + D\sin(4) + E\cos(8) + F\sin(8) + G\cos(12) + H\sin(12) \amp . \end{alignedat}\right.\] All of the terms in these equations are numbers, except for the unknowns \(B,C,D,E,F,G,H\text{:}\) \small\[ \spalignsysdelims.. \left\{\begin{alignedat}{2} -1 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.6536C + 0.7568D - 0.1455E - 0.9894F + 0.8439G + 0.5366H \amp {} \\ 0 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.9900C - 0.1411D + 0.9602E + 0.2794F - 0.9111G - 0.4121H \amp {} \\ -1.5 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.4161C - 0.9093D - 0.6536E + 0.7568F + 0.9602G + 0.2794H \amp {} \\ 0.5 \amp {}={} \amp B + 0.5403C - 0.8415D - 0.4161E - 0.9093F - 0.9900G - 0.1411H \amp {} \\ 1 \amp {}={} \amp B + C \. + E \. + G \amp {} \\ -1 \amp {}={} \amp B + 0.5403C + 0.8415D - 0.4161E + 0.9093F - 0.9900G + 0.1411H \amp {} \\ -0.5 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.4161C + 0.9093D - 0.6536E - 0.7568F + 0.9602G - 0.2794H \amp {} \\ 2 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.9900C + 0.1411D + 0.9602E - 0.2794F - 0.9111G + 0.4121H \amp {} \\ -1 \amp {}={} \amp B - 0.6536C - 0.7568D - 0.1455E + 0.9894F + 0.8439G - 0.5366H \amp . \end{alignedat}\right.\] Hence we want to solve the least-squares problem \small\[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp -0.6536 \amp 0.7568 \amp -0.1455 \amp -0.9894 \amp 0.8439 \amp 0.5366 \\ 1 \amp -0.9900 \amp -0.1411 \amp 0.9602 \amp 0.2794 \amp -0.9111 \amp -0.4121 \\ 1 \amp -0.4161 \amp -0.9093 \amp -0.6536 \amp 0.7568 \amp 0.9602 \amp 0.2794 \\ 1 \amp 0.5403 \amp -0.8415 \amp -0.4161 \amp -0.9093 \amp -0.9900 \amp -0.1411 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 0 \amp 1 \amp 0 \amp 1 \amp 0 \\ 1 \amp 0.5403 \amp 0.8415 \amp -0.4161 \amp 0.9093 \amp -0.9900 \amp 0.1411 \\ 1 \amp -0.4161 \amp 0.9093 \amp -0.6536 \amp -0.7568 \amp 0.9602 \amp -0.2794 \\ 1 \amp -0.9900 \amp 0.1411 \amp 0.9602 \amp -0.2794 \amp -0.9111 \amp 0.4121 \\ 1 \amp -0.6536 \amp -0.7568 \amp -0.1455 \amp 0.9894 \amp 0.8439 \amp -0.5366 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} B \\ C \\ D \\ E \\ F \\ G \\ H \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ -1.5 \\ 0.5 \\ 1 \\ -1 \\ -0.5 \\ 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}. \] We find the least-squares solution with the aid of a computer: \small\[ \hat x \approx \begin{pmatrix} -0.1435 \\ 0.2611 \\ -0.2337 \\ 1.116 \\ -0.5997 \\ -0.2767 \\ 0.1076 \end{pmatrix}. \] Therefore, the best-fit function is
\begin{align*} y \amp\approx -0.1435 + 0.2611\cos(x) -0.2337\sin(x) + 1.116\cos(2x) -0.5997\sin(2x)\\ \amp\qquad\qquad -0.2767\cos(3x) + 0.1076\sin(3x)\text{.} \end{align*}
