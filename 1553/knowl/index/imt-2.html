Theorem 5.1.31.

Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix, and let \(T\colon\R^n\to\R^n\) be the matrix transformation \(T(x) = Ax\text{.}\) The following statements are equivalent:
  1. \(A\) is invertible.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(A\) has \(n\) pivots.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\Nul(A) = \{0\}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The columns of \(A\) are linearly independent.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. The columns of \(A\) span \(\R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(Ax=b\) has a unique solution for each \(b\) in \(\R^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \(T\) is invertible.
    🔗
    🔗
  8. \(T\) is one-to-one.
    🔗
    🔗
  9. \(T\) is onto.
    🔗
    🔗
  10. \(\det(A) \neq 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  11. \(0\) is not an eigenvalue of \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
