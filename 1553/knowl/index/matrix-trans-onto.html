Theorem 3.2.19 Onto matrix transformations.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix, and let \(T(x)=Ax\) be the associated matrix transformation. The following statements are equivalent:
  1. \(T\) is onto.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(T(x)=b\) has at least one solution for every \(b\) in \(\R^m\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(Ax=b\) is consistent for every \(b\) in \(\R^m\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. The columns of \(A\) span \(\R^m\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(A\) has a pivot in every row.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. The range of \(T\) has dimension \(m\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Statements 1, 2, and 3 are translations of each other. The equivalence of 3, 4, 5, and 6 follows from Theorem 16 of Section 2.3.
