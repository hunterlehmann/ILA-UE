Example 6.2.19 Orthogonal complement of an eigenspace.

Find the orthogonal complement of the \(5\)-eigenspace of the matrix
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp 4 \amp -1 \\ 3 \amp 2 \amp 0 \\ -2 \amp 4 \amp 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗

Solution.

The \(5\)-eigenspace is
\begin{align*} W = \Nul(A - 5I_3) = \Nul\begin{pmatrix} -3 \amp 4 \amp -1 \\ 3 \amp -3 \amp 0 \\ -2 \amp 4 \amp -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{,} \end{align*}
so
\begin{align*} W^\perp = \Row\begin{pmatrix} -3 \amp 4 \amp -1 \\ 3 \amp -3 \amp 0 \\ -2 \amp 4 \amp -2 \end{pmatrix} = \Span\left\{\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 4 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\right\}\text{.} \end{align*}
