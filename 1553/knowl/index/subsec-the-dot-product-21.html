The standard coordinate vectors \(e_1,e_2,e_3,\ldots\) are unit vectors:
\begin{gather*} \|e_1\| = \left\|\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\right\| = \sqrt{1^2 + 0^2 + 0^2} = 1\text{.} \end{gather*}
For any nonzero vector \(x\text{,}\) there is a unique unit vector pointing in the same direction. It is obtained by dividing by the length of \(x\text{.}\)
