Example 5.3.31.

Consider the matrices
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1/2 \amp 3/2 \\ 3/2 \amp 1/2 \end{pmatrix} \qquad D = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad C = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
One can verify that \(A = CDC\inv\text{:}\) see Example 15. Let \(v_1 = {1\choose 1}\) and \(v_2 = {1\choose -1}\text{,}\) the columns of \(C\text{.}\) These are eigenvectors of \(A\text{,}\) with corresponding eigenvalues \(2\) and \(-1\text{.}\)
🔗
The matrix \(D\) is diagonal: it scales the \(x\)-direction by a factor of \(2\) and the \(y\)-direction by a factor of \(-1\text{.}\)

🔗
If we write a vector in terms of the basis \(v_1,v_2\text{,}\) say, \(x = a_1v_1 + a_2v_2,\) then it is easy to compute \(Ax\text{:}\)
\begin{gather*} Ax = A(a_1v_1 + a_2v_2) = a_1Av_1 + a_2Av_2 = 2a_1v_1 -a_2v_2\text{.} \end{gather*}
Here we have used the fact that \(v_1,v_2\) are eigenvectors of \(A\text{.}\) Since the resulting vector is still expressed in terms of the basis \(v_1,v_2\text{,}\) we can visualize what \(A\) does to the vector \(x\text{:}\) it scales the “\(v_1\)-coordinate” by \(2\) and the “\(v_2\)-coordinate” by \(-1\text{.}\)
🔗
For instance, let \(\textcolor{seq-red}x = {0\choose -2}\text{.}\) We see from the grid on the right in the picture below that \(x = -v_1 + v_2\text{,}\) so
\begin{gather*} Ax = A(-v_1+v_2) = -Av_1 + Av_2 = -2v_1 - v_2 = -2\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
The picture illustrates the action of \(D\) on the plane in the usual basis, and the action of \(A\) on the plane in the \(v_1,v_2\)-basis.
🔗

🔗
Now let \(\textcolor{seq-red}x = \frac 12{5\choose -3}\text{.}\) We see from the grid on the right in the picture below that \(x = \frac 12v_1 + 2v_2\text{,}\) so
\begin{gather*} Ax = A\left(\frac 12v_1 + 2v_2\right) = \frac 12Av_1 + 2Av_2 = v_1 - 2v_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} - 2\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
