Proposition 6.3.35 Properties of Projection Matrices.

Let \(W\) be a subspace of \(\R^n\text{,}\) define \(T\colon\R^n\to\R^n\) by \(T(x) = x_W\text{,}\) and let \(B\) be the standard matrix for \(T\text{.}\) Then:
  1. \(\displaystyle \Col(B) = W.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \Nul(B) = W^\perp.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\displaystyle B^2 = B.\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. If \(W \neq \{0\}\text{,}\) then 1 is an eigenvalue of \(B\) and the 1-eigenspace for \(B\) is \(W\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. If \(W \neq \R^n\text{,}\) then 0 is an eigenvalue of \(B\) and the 0-eigenspace for \(B\) is \(W^\perp\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(B\) is similar to the diagonal matrix with \(m\) ones and \(n-m\) zeros on the diagonal, where \(m = \dim(W).\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The first four assertions are translations of Proposition 31, respectively, using Insight 23 of Section 3.1 and Theorem 25 of Section 3.4. The fifth assertion is equivalent to the second, by Fact 30 of Section 5.1.
🔗
For the final assertion, we showed in the proof of Theorem 2 that there is a basis of \(\R^n\) of the form \(\{v_1,\ldots,v_m,v_{m+1},\ldots,v_n\}\text{,}\) where \(\{v_1,\ldots,v_m\}\) is a basis for \(W\) and \(\{v_{m+1},\ldots,v_n\}\) is a basis for \(W^\perp\text{.}\) Each \(v_i\) is an eigenvector of \(B\text{:}\) indeed, for \(i\leq m\) we have
\begin{gather*} Bv_i = T(v_i) = v_i = 1\cdot v_i \end{gather*}
because \(v_i\) is in \(W\text{,}\) and for \(i > m\) we have
\begin{gather*} Bv_i = T(v_i) = 0 = 0\cdot v_i \end{gather*}
because \(v_i\) is in \(W^\perp\text{.}\) Therefore, we have found a basis of eigenvectors, with associated eigenvalues \(1,\ldots,1,0,\ldots,0\) (\(m\) ones and \(n-m\) zeros). Now we use the Theorem 11 of Section 5.3.
