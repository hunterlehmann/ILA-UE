Example 3.1.15 Shear.

Let
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Describe the function \(b=Ax\) geometrically.
🔗

Solution.

In the equation \(Ax=b\text{,}\) the input vector \(x\) and the output vector \(b\) are both in \(\R^2\text{.}\) First we multiply \(A\) by a vector to see what it does:
\begin{align*} A\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x+y \\ y \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Multiplication by \(A\) adds the \(y\)-coordinate to the \(x\)-coordinate; this is called a shear in the \(x\)-direction. \def\sheep{\includegraphics[width=4cm]{sheep.jpg}} \begin{tikzpicture} \node (sheep1) at (0,0,0) {\sheep}; \draw[->,opacity=.6] (-1.3,0) -- (1.3,0); \draw[->,opacity=.6] (0,-1.3) -- (0,1.3); \begin{scope}[xshift=7cm] \node[cm={1,0,1,1,(0,0)}] (sheep2) at (0,0,0) {\sheep}; \draw[->,opacity=.6] (-1.3,0) -- (1.3,0); \draw[->,opacity=.6] (0,-1.3) -- (0,1.3); \end{scope} \draw[->] ($(sheep1.east)+(0,.3)$) to[bend left, "$b = Ax$"] ($(sheep2.west)+(0,.3)$); \end{tikzpicture}
