Definition 6.4.1.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix and let \(b\) be a vector in \(\R^m\text{.}\) A least-squares solution of the matrix equation \(Ax=b\) is a vector \(\hat x\) in \(\R^n\) such that
\begin{gather*} \dist(b,\,A\hat x) \leq \dist(b,\,Ax) \end{gather*}
for all other vectors \(x\) in \(\R^n\text{.}\)
