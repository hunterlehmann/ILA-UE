Definition 3.4.33.

  • Let \(T,U\colon\R^n\to\R^m\) be two transformations. Their sum is the transformation \(T+U\colon\R^n\to\R^m\) defined by
    \begin{gather*} (T+U)(x) = T(x) + U(x)\text{.} \end{gather*}
    Note that addition of transformations is only defined when both transformations have the same domain and codomain.

  • Let \(T\colon\R^n\to\R^m\) be a transformation, and let \(c\) be a scalar. The scalar product of \(c\) with \(T\) is the transformation \(cT\colon\R^n\to\R^m\) defined by
    \begin{gather*} (cT)(x) = c\cdot T(x)\text{.} \end{gather*}
