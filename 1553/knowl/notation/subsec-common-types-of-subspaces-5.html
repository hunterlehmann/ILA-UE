Definition 2.6.15.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix.
  • The column space of \(A\) is the subspace of \(\R^m\) spanned by the columns of \(A\text{.}\) It is written \(\Col(A)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • The null space of \(A\) is the subspace of \(\R^n\) consisting of all solutions of the homogeneous equation \(Ax=0\text{:}\)
    \begin{gather*} \Nul(A) = \bigl\{ x \text{ in } \R^n\bigm| Ax=0 \bigr\}\text{.} \end{gather*}
