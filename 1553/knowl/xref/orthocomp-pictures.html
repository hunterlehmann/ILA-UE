Example 6.2.9.

The orthogonal complement of \(\R^n\) is \(\{0\}\text{,}\) since the zero vector is the only vector that is orthogonal to all of the vectors in \(\R^n\text{.}\)
🔗
For the same reason, we have \(\{0\}^\perp = \R^n\text{.}\)
