Example 1.3.1 A System with a Free Variable.

Consider the linear system
\begin{align*} \left\{\begin{alignedat}{4} 2x \amp {}+{} \amp y \amp {}+{} \amp 12z \amp {}={} \amp 1 \amp {} \\ x \amp {}+{} \amp 2y \amp {}+{} \amp 9z \amp {}={} \amp -1 \amp . \end{alignedat}\right. \end{align*}
We solve it using row reduction:
\begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} 2 \amp 1 \amp 12 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \amp 9 \amp -1 \end{array}\right) \quad\xrightarrow{R_1 \longleftrightarrow R_2}\quad \amp\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} \mh{1} \amp 2 \amp 9 \amp -1 \\ 2 \amp 1 \amp 12 \amp 1 \end{array}\right) \amp\amp\mhb{\text{(Optional)}}\\ {}\quad\xrightarrow{R_2=R_2-2R_1}\quad \amp\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} 1 \amp 2 \amp 9 \amp -1 \\ \mh{0} \amp -3 \amp -6 \amp 3 \end{array}\right) \amp\amp\mhb{\text{(Step 1c)}}\\ {}\quad\xrightarrow{R_2=R_2\div -3}\quad \amp\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} 1 \amp 2 \amp 9 \amp -1 \\ 0 \amp \mh{1} \amp 2 \amp -1 \end{array}\right) \amp\amp\mhb{\text{(Step 2b)}}\\ {}\quad\xrightarrow{R_1=R_1-2R_2}\quad \amp\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r} 1 \amp \mh{0} \amp 5 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 2 \amp -1 \end{array}\right) \amp\amp\mhb{\text{(Step 2c)}} \end{align*}
This row reduced matrix corresponds to the linear system
\begin{align*} \left\{\begin{alignedat}{3} x \amp {}+{} \amp 5z \amp {}={} \amp 1 \amp {} \\ y \amp {}+{} \amp 2z \amp {}={} \amp -1 \amp . \end{alignedat}\right. \end{align*}
In what sense is the system solved? We rewrite as
\begin{align*} \left\{\begin{alignedat}{2} x \amp {}={} \amp 1 - 5z \amp {} \\ y \amp {}={} \amp -1 - 2z \amp . \end{alignedat}\right. \end{align*}
For any value of \(z\text{,}\) there is exactly one value of \(x\) and \(y\) that make the equations true. But we are free to choose any value of \(z\text{.}\)
🔗
We have found all solutions: it is the set of all values \(x,y,z\text{,}\) where
\begin{align*} \left\{\begin{alignedat}{2} x \amp {}={} \amp 1 - 5z \amp {} \\ y \amp {}={} \amp -1 - 2z \amp {} \\ z \amp {}={} \amp z \amp {} \end{alignedat}\right. \qquad \text{$z$ any real number.} \end{align*}
This is called the parametric form for the solution to the linear system. The variable \(z\) is called a free variable.
