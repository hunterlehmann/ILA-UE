Recipe 2.3.10 The row-column rule for matrix-vector multiplication.

If \(A\) is an \(m\times n\) matrix with rows \(r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_m\text{,}\) and \(x\) is a vector in \(\R^n\text{,}\) then
\begin{gather*} Ax = \left(\begin{array}{c} \matrow{r_1} \\ \matrow{r_2} \\ \vdots \\ \matrow{r_m} \end{array}\right) x = \begin{pmatrix} r_1x \\ r_2x \\ \vdots \\ r_mx \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
