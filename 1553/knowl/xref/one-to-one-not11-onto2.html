Example 3.2.20 A matrix transformation that is onto.

Let \(A\) be the matrix
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{,} \end{align*}
and define \(T\colon\R^3\to\R^2\) by \(T(x) = Ax\text{.}\) Is \(T\) onto?
🔗

Solution.

The reduced row echelon form of \(A\) is
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \amp -1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Hence \(A\) has a pivot in every row, so \(T\) is onto.
