Example 2.7.13 A basis of a span.

Find a basis of the subspace
\begin{gather*} V = \Span\left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 4 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\right\}\text{.} \end{gather*}
🔗

Solution.

The subspace \(V\) is the column space of the matrix
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 2 \amp 0 \amp -1 \\ -2 \amp -3 \amp 4 \amp 5 \\ 2 \amp 4 \amp 0 \amp -2 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
The reduced row echelon form of this matrix is
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \amp -8 \amp -7 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 4 \amp 3 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp 0 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
The first two columns are pivot columns, so a basis for \(V\) is
\begin{gather*} \left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}\right\}\text{.} \end{gather*}
