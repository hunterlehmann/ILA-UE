Insight 3.3.11 Standard coordinate vectors.

The standard coordinate vectors in \(\R^n\) are the \(n\) vectors
\begin{gather*} e_1=\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\quad e_2=\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\quad\ldots,\quad e_{n-1}=\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\quad e_n=\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
The \(i\)th entry of \(e_i\) is equal to 1, and the other entries are zero.
🔗
From now on, for the rest of the book, we will use the symbols \(\mh{e_1,e_2,\ldots}\) to denote the standard coordinate vectors.
