Definition 2.3.9.

A row vector is a matrix with one row. The product of a row vector of length \(n\) and a (column) vector of length \(n\) is
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} a_1 \amp a_2 \amp \cdots \amp a_n \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} = a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \cdots + a_nx_n\text{.} \end{align*}
This is a scalar.
