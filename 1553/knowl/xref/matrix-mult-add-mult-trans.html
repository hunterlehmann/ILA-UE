Note 3.4.35 Properties of addition and scalar multiplication for transformations.

Let \(S,T,U\colon\R^n\to\R^m\) be transformations and let \(c,d\) be scalars. The following properties are easily verified:
\begin{align*} T + U \amp= U + T \amp S + (T + U) \amp= (S + T) + U\\ c(T + U) \amp= cT + cU \amp (c + d)T \amp= cT + dT\\ c(dT) \amp= (cd)T \amp T + 0 \amp= T \end{align*}
