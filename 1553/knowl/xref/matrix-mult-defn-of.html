Definition 3.4.17 Matrix multiplication.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix and let \(B\) be an \(n\times p\) matrix. Denote the columns of \(B\) by \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_p\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} B = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \amp \cdots \amp v_p \\ | \amp | \amp | \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
The product \(AB\) is the \(m\times p\) matrix with columns \(Av_1,Av_2,\ldots,Av_p\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} AB = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \amp | \\ Av_1 \amp Av_2 \amp \cdots \amp Av_p \\ | \amp | \amp | \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
