Example 3.3.19 A transformation defined by a formula.

Define \(T\colon\R^2\to\R^3\) by the formula
\begin{gather*} T\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3x-y \\ y \\ x \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
Find the standard matrix for \(T\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

We substitute the standard coordinate vectors into the formula defining \(T\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \left.\begin{aligned} T(e_1) \amp= T\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3(1)-0 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \\ T(e_2) \amp= T\begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3(0)-1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \end{aligned}\right\} \implies A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \amp -1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
