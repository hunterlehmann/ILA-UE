Note 2.33 Three characterizations of consistency.

Now we have three equivalent ways of making the same statement:
  1. A vector \(b\) is in the span of \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The vector equation
    \begin{gather*} x_1v_1 + x_2v_2 + \cdots + x_kv_k = b \end{gather*}
    has a solution.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The linear system with augmented matrix
    \begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{cccc|c} | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \amp \cdots \amp v_k \amp b \\ | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \amp | \end{array}\right) \end{align*}
    is consistent.
    🔗
    🔗
