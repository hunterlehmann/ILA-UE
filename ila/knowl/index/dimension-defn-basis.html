Definition 2.7.1.

Let \(V\) be a subspace of \(\R^n\text{.}\) A basis of \(V\) is a set of vectors \(\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m\}\) in \(V\) such that:
  1. \(V = \Span\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m\}\text{,}\) and
    🔗
    🔗
  2. the set \(\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m\}\) is linearly independent.
