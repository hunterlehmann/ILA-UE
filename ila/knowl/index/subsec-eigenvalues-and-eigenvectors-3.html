Definition 5.1.1.

Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix.
  1. An eigenvector of \(A\) is a nonzero vector \(v\) in \(\R^n\) such that \(Av=\lambda v\text{,}\) for some scalar \(\lambda\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. An eigenvalue of \(A\) is a scalar \(\lambda\) such that the equation \(Av=\lambda v\) has a nontrivial solution.
    🔗
    🔗
If \(Av = \lambda v\) for \(v\neq 0\text{,}\) we say that \(\lambda\) is the eigenvalue for \(v\text{,}\) and that \(v\) is an eigenvector for \(\lambda\text{.}\)
