Note that the signs of the cofactors follow a “checkerboard pattern.” Namely, \((-1)^{i+j}\) is pictured in this matrix:
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \\ \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \\ \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \\ \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \amp \mhb{-} \amp \mhg{+} \\ \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
