Note 6.1.2 Properties of the Dot Product.

Let \(x,y,z\) be vectors in \(\R^n\) and let \(c\) be a scalar.
  1. Commutativity: \(x\cdot y = y \cdot x\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Distributivity with addition: \((x + y)\cdot z = x\cdot z+y\cdot z\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Distributivity with scalar multiplication: \((cx)\cdot y = c(x\cdot y)\text{.}\)
