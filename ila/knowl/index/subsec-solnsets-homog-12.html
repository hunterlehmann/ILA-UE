Recipe 2.4.6 Parametric vector form (homogeneous case).

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix. Suppose that the free variables in the homogeneous equation \(Ax=0\) are, for example, \(x_3\text{,}\) \(x_6\text{,}\) and \(x_8\text{.}\)
  1. Find the reduced row echelon form of \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Write the parametric form of the solution set, including the redundant equations \(x_3=x_3\text{,}\) \(x_6=x_6\text{,}\) \(x_8=x_8\text{.}\) Put equations for all of the \(x_i\) in order.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Make a single vector equation from these equations by making the coefficients of \(x_3,x_6,\) and \(x_8\) into vectors \(v_3,v_6,\) and \(v_8\text{,}\) respectively.
    🔗
    🔗
The solutions to \(Ax=0\) will then be expressed in the form
\begin{gather*} x = x_3v_3 + x_6v_6 + x_8v_8 \end{gather*}
for some vectors \(v_3,v_6,v_8\) in \(\R^n\text{,}\) and any scalars \(x_3,x_6,x_8\text{.}\) This is called the parametric vector form of the solution.
🔗
In this case, the solution set can be written as \(\Span\{v_3,\,v_6,\,v_8\}.\)
