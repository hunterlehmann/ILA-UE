Recipes: \(\cB\)-coordinates.

If \(\cB = \{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m\}\) is a basis for a subspace \(V\) and \(x\) is in \(V\text{,}\) then
\begin{gather*} [x]_\cB = \begin{pmatrix} c_1 \\ c_2 \\ \vdots \\ c_m \end{pmatrix} \sptxt{means} x = c_1v_1 + c_2v_2 + \cdots + c_mv_m\text{.} \end{gather*}
Finding the \(\cB\)-coordinates of \(x\) means solving the vector equation
\begin{gather*} x = c_1v_1 + c_2v_2 + \cdots + c_mv_m \end{gather*}
in the unknowns \(c_1,c_2,\ldots,c_m\text{.}\) This generally means row reducing the augmented matrix
\begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{cccc|c} | \amp | \amp | \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \amp \cdots \amp v_m \amp x \\ | \amp | \amp | \amp | \end{array}\right)\text{.} \end{align*}
