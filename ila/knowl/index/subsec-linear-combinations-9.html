Example 2.1.20 Linear Combinations of Collinear Vectors.

The set of all linear combinations of the vectors
\begin{gather*} v_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \sptxt{and} v_2 = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} \end{gather*}
is the line containing both vectors.
🔗

🔗
The difference between this and the previous Example 15 is that both vectors lie on the same line. Hence any scalar multiples of \(v_1,v_2\) lie on that line, as does their sum.
