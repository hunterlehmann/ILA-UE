Insight 5.4.25 Diagonalizability has nothing to do with invertibility.

Of the following matrices, the first is diagonalizable and invertible, the second is diagonalizable but not invertible, the third is invertible but not diagonalizable, and the fourth is neither invertible nor diagonalizable, as the reader can verify:
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix} \qquad \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad \begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
