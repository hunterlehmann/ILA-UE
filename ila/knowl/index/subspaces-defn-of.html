Definition 2.6.2.

A subspace of \(\R^n\) is a subset \(V\) of \(\R^n\) satisfying:
  1. Non-emptiness: The zero vector is in \(V\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Closure under addition: If \(u\) and \(v\) are in \(V\text{,}\) then \(u+v\) is also in \(V\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Closure under scalar multiplication: If \(v\) is in \(V\) and \(c\) is in \(\R\text{,}\) then \(cv\) is also in \(V\text{.}\)
