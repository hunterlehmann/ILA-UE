Theorem 4.2.13.

Let \(A\) be an invertible \(n\times n\) matrix, with cofactors \(C_{ij}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{align} A\inv = \frac 1{\det(A)} \begin{pmatrix} C_{11} \amp C_{21} \amp \cdots \amp C_{n-1,1} \amp C_{n1} \\ C_{12} \amp C_{22} \amp \cdots \amp C_{n-1,2} \amp C_{n2} \\ \vdots \amp \vdots \amp \ddots \amp \vdots \amp \vdots \\ C_{1,n-1} \amp C_{2,n-1} \amp \cdots \amp C_{n-1,n-1} \amp C_{n,n-1} \\ C_{1n} \amp C_{2n} \amp \cdots \amp C_{n-1,n} \amp C_{nn} \end{pmatrix}\text{.}\tag{4.2.1} \end{align}
