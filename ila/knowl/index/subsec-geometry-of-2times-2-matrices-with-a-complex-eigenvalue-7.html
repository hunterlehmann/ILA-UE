Recipe 5.5.15 A \(2\times 2\) matrix with a complex eigenvalue.

Let \(A\) be a \(2\times 2\) real matrix.
  1. Compute the characteristic polynomial
    \begin{gather*} f(\lambda) = \lambda^2 - \Tr(A)\lambda + \det(A)\text{,} \end{gather*}
    then compute its roots using the quadratic formula.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If the eigenvalues are complex, choose one of them, and call it \(\lambda\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Find a corresponding (complex) eigenvalue \(v\) using the Note 5.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Then \(A=CBC\inv\) for
    \begin{align*} C = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \\ \Re(v) \amp \Im(v) \\ | \amp | \end{pmatrix} \sptxt{and} B = \begin{pmatrix} \Re(\lambda) \amp \Im(\lambda) \\ -\Im(\lambda) \amp \Re(\lambda) \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
    This scales by a factor of \(|\lambda|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
