Recipe 6.4.15 \(\cB\)-coordinates when \(\cB\) is an orthogonal set.

Let \(W\) be a subspace of \(\R^n\) with orthogonal basis \(\cB=\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m\}\) and let \(x\) be a vector in \(W\text{.}\) Then
\begin{gather*} [x]_\cB = \left( \frac{x\cdot u_1}{u_1\cdot u_1},\; \frac{x\cdot u_2}{u_2\cdot u_2},\; \ldots,\; \frac{x\cdot u_m}{u_m\cdot u_m} \right)\text{.} \end{gather*}
