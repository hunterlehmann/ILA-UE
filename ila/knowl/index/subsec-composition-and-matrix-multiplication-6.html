Example 3.4.27 Composition of rotations.

In Example 18 of Section 3.3, we showed that the standard matrix for the counterclockwise rotation of the plane by an angle of \(\theta\) is
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta \amp -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta \amp \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Let \(T\colon\R^2\to\R^2\) be counterclockwise rotation by \(45^\circ\text{,}\) and let \(U\colon\R^2\to\R^2\) be counterclockwise rotation by \(90^\circ\text{.}\) The matrices \(A\) and \(B\) for \(T\) and \(U\) are, respectively,
\begin{align*} A \amp= \begin{pmatrix} \cos(45^\circ) \amp -\sin(45^\circ) \\ \sin(45^\circ) \amp \cos(45^\circ) \end{pmatrix} = \frac 1{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\\ B \amp= \begin{pmatrix} \cos(90^\circ) \amp -\sin(90^\circ) \\ \sin(90^\circ) \amp \cos(90^\circ) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
Here we used the trigonometric identities
\begin{align*} \cos(45^\circ) \amp= \frac 1{\sqrt2} \amp \sin(45^\circ) \amp= \frac 1{\sqrt2}\\ \cos(90^\circ) \amp= 0 \amp \sin(90^\circ) \amp= 1\text{.} \end{align*}
The standard matrix of the composition \(T\circ U\) is
\begin{align*} AB = \frac 1{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix} = \frac 1{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix} -1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
This is consistent with the fact that \(T\circ U\) is counterclockwise rotation by \(90^\circ + 45^\circ = 135^\circ\text{:}\) we have
\begin{align*} \begin{pmatrix} \cos(135^\circ) \amp -\sin(135^\circ) \\ \sin(135^\circ) \amp \cos(135^\circ) \end{pmatrix} = \frac 1{\sqrt 2}\begin{pmatrix} -1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}
because \(\cos(135^\circ) = -1/\sqrt2\) and \(\sin(135^\circ) = 1/\sqrt2\text{.}\)
