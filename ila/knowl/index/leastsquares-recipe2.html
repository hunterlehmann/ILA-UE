Recipe 6.5.14 Compute a least-squares solution.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix with orthogonal columns \(u_1,u_2,\ldots,u_m\text{,}\) and let \(b\) be a vector in \(\R^n\text{.}\) Then the least-squares solution of \(Ax=b\) is the vector
\begin{gather*} \hat x = \left(\frac{b\cdot u_1}{u_1\cdot u_1},\; \frac{b\cdot u_2}{u_2\cdot u_2},\; \ldots,\; \frac{b\cdot u_m}{u_m\cdot u_m} \right)\text{.} \end{gather*}
