Subsets versus Subspaces.

A subset of \(\R^n\) is any collection of vectors whatsoever. For instance, the unit circle
\begin{gather*} C = \bigl\{ (x,y)\text{ in }\R^2 \bigm| x^2 + y^2 = 1 \bigr\} \end{gather*}
is a subset of \(\R^2\text{,}\) but it is not a subspace. In fact, all of the non-examples above are still subsets of \(\R^n\text{.}\) A subspace is a subset that happens to satisfy the three additional defining properties.
