Example 5.1.13 Dilation.

Let \(T\colon\R^2\to\R^2\) be the linear transformation that dilates by a factor of \(1.5\text{,}\) and let \(A\) be the matrix for \(T\text{.}\) Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of \(A\) without doing any computations.
🔗

Solution.

We have
\begin{gather*} Av = T(v) = 1.5v \end{gather*}
for every vector \(v\) in \(\R^2\text{.}\) Therefore, by definition every nonzero vector is an eigenvector with eigenvalue \(1.5.\)
