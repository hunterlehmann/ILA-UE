Example A.0.4 Degree-2 Polynomials.

The quadratic formula gives the roots of a degree-2 polynomial, real or complex:
\begin{gather*} f(x) = x^2 + bx + c \implies x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4c}}2\text{.} \end{gather*}
🔗
For example, if \(f(x) = x^2 - \sqrt 2x + 1\text{,}\) then
\begin{gather*} x = \frac{\sqrt 2\pm\sqrt{-2}}2 = \frac{\sqrt 2}2(1\pm i) = \frac{1\pm i}{\sqrt 2}\text{.} \end{gather*}
Note that if \(b,c\) are real numbers, then the two roots are complex conjugates.
