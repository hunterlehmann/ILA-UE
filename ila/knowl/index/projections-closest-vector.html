Closest vector and distance.

Let \(W\) be a subspace of \(\R^n\) and let \(x\) be a vector in \(\R^n\text{.}\)
  • The orthogonal projection \(x_W\) is the closest vector to \(x\) in \(W\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • The distance from \(x\) to \(W\) is \(\|x_{W^\perp}\|\text{.}\)
