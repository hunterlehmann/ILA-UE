Example 4.3.7 Length.

A \(1\times 1\) matrix \(A\) is just a number \(\mat a\text{.}\) In this case, the parallelepiped \(P\) determined by its one row is just the interval \([0,a]\) (or \([a,0]\) if \(a\lt0\)). The “volume” of a region in \(\R^1 = \R\) is just its length, so it is clear in this case that \(\vol(P) = |a|\text{.}\)
