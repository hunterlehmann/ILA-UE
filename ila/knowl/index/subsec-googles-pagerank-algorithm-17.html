Definition 5.6.20 The Google Matrix.

Let \(A\) be the importance matrix for an internet with \(n\) pages, and let \(A'\) be the modified importance matrix. The Google Matrix is the matrix
\begin{align*} M = (1-p)\cdot A' + p\cdot B \sptxt{where} B = \frac 1n\begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \amp \cdots \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp \cdots \amp 1 \\ \vdots \amp \vdots \amp \ddots \amp \vdots \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp \cdots \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
