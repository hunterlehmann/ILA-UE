Definition 2.3.2.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix with columns \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_n\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \amp \cdots \amp v_n \\ | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}
The product of \(A\) with a vector \(x\) in \(\R^n\) is the linear combination
\begin{align*} Ax = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \amp \cdots \amp v_n \\ | \amp | \amp {} \amp | \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} = x_1v_1 + x_2v_2 + \cdots + x_nv_n\text{.} \end{align*}
This is a vector in \(\R^m\text{.}\)
