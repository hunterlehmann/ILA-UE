Example 3.5.22 Reflection.

Let \(T\colon\R^2\to\R^2\) be the reflection over the \(y\)-axis. Is \(T\) invertible? If so, what is \(T\inv\text{?}\)
🔗

Solution.

The transformation \(T\) is invertible; in fact, it is equal to its own inverse.
