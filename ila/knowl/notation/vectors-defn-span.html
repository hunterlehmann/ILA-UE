Definition 2.29.

Let \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\) be vectors in \(\R^n\text{.}\) The span of \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\) is the collection of all linear combinations of \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\text{,}\) and is denoted \(\Span\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\}\text{.}\) In symbols:
\begin{gather*} \Span\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\} = \bigl\{x_1v_1 + x_2v_2 + \cdots + x_kv_k \mid x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_k \text{ in }\R\bigr\} \end{gather*}
We also say that \(\Span\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\}\) is the subset spanned by or generated by the vectors \(v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_k\text{.}\)
