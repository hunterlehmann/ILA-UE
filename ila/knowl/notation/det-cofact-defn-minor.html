Definition 4.2.1.

Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix.
  1. The \((i,j)\) minor, denoted \(A_{ij}\text{,}\) is the \((n-1)\times (n-1)\) matrix obtained from \(A\) by deleting the \(i\)th row and the \(j\)th column.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The \((i,j)\) cofactor \(C_{ij}\) is defined in terms of the minor by
    \begin{gather*} C_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}\det(A_{ij})\text{.} \end{gather*}
