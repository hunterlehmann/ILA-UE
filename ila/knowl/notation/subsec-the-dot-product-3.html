Definition 6.1.1.

The dot product of two vectors \(x,y\) in \(\R^n\) is
\begin{gather*} x\cdot y = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} y_1 \\ y_2 \\ \vdots \\ y_n \end{pmatrix} \;=\; x_1y_1 + x_2y_2 + \cdots + x_ny_n\text{.} \end{gather*}
Thinking of \(x,y\) as column vectors, this is the same as \(x^Ty\text{.}\)
