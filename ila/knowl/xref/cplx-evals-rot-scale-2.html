Example 5.5.11 A rotation-scaling matrix.

What does the matrix
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} -\sqrt3 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp -\sqrt3 \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}
do geometrically?
🔗

Solution.

This is a rotation-scaling matrix with \(a=-\sqrt3\) and \(b=1\text{.}\) Therefore, it scales by a factor of \(\sqrt{\det(A)}=\sqrt{3+1}=2\) and rotates counterclockwise by the angle \(\theta\) in the picture: \usetikzlibrary{angles}
