Example 5.5.9 A rotation-scaling matrix.

What does the matrix
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}
do geometrically?
🔗

Solution.

This is a rotation-scaling matrix with \(a=b=1\text{.}\) Therefore, it scales by a factor of \(\sqrt{\det(A)} = \sqrt 2\) and rotates counterclockwise by \(45^\circ\text{:}\) \usetikzlibrary{angles}
