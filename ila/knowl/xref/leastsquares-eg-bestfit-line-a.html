Example 6.5.7.

Find the least-squares solutions of \(Ax=b\) where:
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \\ 2 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad b = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
What quantity is being minimized?
🔗

Solution.

We have
\begin{align*} A^TA = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \amp 2 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \\ 2 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \amp 3 \\ 3 \amp 3 \end{pmatrix} \end{align*}
and
\begin{align*} A^T b = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \amp 1 \amp 2 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
We form an augmented matrix and row reduce:
\begin{align*} \left(\begin{array}{rr|r} 5 \amp 3 \amp 0 \\ 3 \amp 3 \amp 6 \end{array}\right) \rref \left(\begin{array}{rr|r} 1 \amp 0 \amp -3 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 5 \end{array}\right)\text{.} \end{align*}
Therefore, the only least-squares solution is \(\hat x = {-3\choose 5}.\)
🔗
This solution minimizes the distance from \(A\hat x\) to \(b\text{,}\) i.e., the sum of the squares of the entries of \(b-A\hat x = b-b_{\Col(A)} = b_{\Col(A)^\perp}\text{.}\) In this case, we have
\begin{gather*} \|b-A\hat x\| = \left\|\begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}\right\| = \left\|\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\right\| = \sqrt{1^2+(-2)^2+1^2} = \sqrt 6\text{.} \end{gather*}
Therefore, \(b_{\Col(A)} = A\hat x\) is \(\sqrt 6\) units from \(b.\)
