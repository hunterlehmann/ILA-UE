Example 6.3.18 Projection onto a line in \(\R^2\).

Compute the orthogonal projection of \(x = {-6\choose 4}\) onto the line \(L\) spanned by \(u = {3\choose 2}\text{,}\) and find the distance from \(x\) to \(L\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

First we find
\begin{gather*} x_L = \frac{x\cdot u}{u\cdot u}\,u = \frac{-18+8}{9+4}\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = -\frac{10}{13}\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} \qquad x_{L^\perp} = x - x_L = \frac 1{13}\begin{pmatrix} -48 \\ 72 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
The distance from \(x\) to \(L\) is
\begin{gather*} \|x_{L^\perp}\| = \frac 1{13}\sqrt{48^2 + 72^2} \approx 6.656\text{.} \end{gather*}
