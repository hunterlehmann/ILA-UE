Definition 3.1.22.

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix. The matrix transformation associated to \(A\) is the transformation
\begin{gather*} T\colon \R^n\To\R^m \sptxt{defined by} T(x) = Ax\text{.} \end{gather*}
This is the transformation that takes a vector \(x\) in \(\R^n\) to the vector \(Ax\) in \(\R^m\text{.}\)
