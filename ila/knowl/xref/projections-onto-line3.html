Example 6.3.20 Projection onto a line in \(\R^3\).

Let
\begin{gather*} x = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad u = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}\text{,} \end{gather*}
and let \(L\) be the line spanned by \(u\text{.}\) Compute \(x_L\) and \(x_L^\perp\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

\begin{gather*} x_L = \frac{x\cdot u}{u\cdot u}\,u = \frac{2+3-1}{1+1+1}\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{4}{3}\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \qquad x_{L^\perp} = x - x_L = \frac 13\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 5 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
