Example 6.3.30 Computing a projection.

Continuing with the above Example 24, let
\begin{gather*} W = \Span\left\{\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix},\;\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}\right\} \qquad x = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{gather*}
Compute \(x_W\) using the formula \(x_W = A(A^TA)\inv A^Tx\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

Clearly the spanning vectors are noncollinear, so according to the Corollary 29, we have \(x_W = A(A^TA)\inv A^Tx\text{,}\) where
\begin{align*} A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\text{.} \end{align*}
We compute
\begin{align*} A^TA = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp 1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \end{pmatrix} \implies (A^TA)\inv = \frac 13\begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp -1 \\ -1 \amp 2 \end{pmatrix}\text{,} \end{align*}
so
\begin{align*} \spalignsysdelims()\spalignsystabspace=0pt x_W \amp= A(A^TA)\inv A^Tx = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\frac 13\begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp -1 \\ -1 \amp 2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 0 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{pmatrix}\\ \amp= \frac 13\begin{pmatrix} 2 \amp 1 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 2 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp 1 \amp 2 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{pmatrix} = \frac 13\left\{\begin{alignedat}{3} 2x_1 \amp {}+{} \amp x_2 \amp {}-{} \amp x_3 \amp {} \\ x_1 \amp {}+{} \amp 2x_2 \amp {}+{} \amp x_3 \amp {} \\ -x_1 \amp {}+{} \amp x_2 \amp {}+{} \amp 2x_3 \amp {} \end{alignedat}\right.\text{.} \end{align*}
So, for example, if \(x=(1,0,0)\text{,}\) this formula tells us that \(x_W = (2,1,-1)\text{.}\)
