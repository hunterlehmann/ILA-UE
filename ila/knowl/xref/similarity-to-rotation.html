A Matrix Similar to a Rotation Matrix.

Let
\begin{align*} B = \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta \amp -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta \amp \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \qquad C = \begin{pmatrix} | \amp | \\ v_1 \amp v_2 \\ | \amp | \end{pmatrix} \qquad A = CBC\inv\text{,} \end{align*}
where \(C\) is assumed invertible. Then:
  • \(B\) rotates the plane by an angle of \(\theta\) around the circle centered at the origin and passing through \(e_1\) and \(e_2\text{,}\) in the direction from \(e_1\) to \(e_2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  • \(A\) rotates the plane by an angle of \(\theta\) around the ellipse centered at the origin and passing through \(v_1\) and \(v_2\text{,}\) in the direction from \(v_1\) to \(v_2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
