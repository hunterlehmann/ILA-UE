    1.1.4.14.

    Solution.
    While Alice and Bob are alive, the difference between their ages is some constant \(c\) no matter what year we are in:
    \begin{gather*} (\textup{Alice's age}) - (\textup{Bob's age}) = c\text{.} \end{gather*}
    We’re told that alice is 24, so
    \begin{gather*} 24-(\textup{Bob's age now}) = c\text{.} \end{gather*}
    Also, from the information we are given, Bob was 12 when Alice was the same age that Bob is now.
    \begin{gather*} (\textup{Alice's age when Bob was 12}) - 12 = c, \quad \textup{ so} \quad (\textup{Bob's age now})-12 = c\text{.} \end{gather*}
    We have a system of two equations:
    \begin{gather*} 24-(\textup{Bob's age now}) = c \end{gather*}
    \begin{gather*} (\textup{Bob's age now}) - 12 = c\text{.} \end{gather*}
    Subtracting the first equation from the second, we get
    \begin{gather*} -36 + 2(\textup{Bob's age now}) = 0\text{,} \end{gather*}
    therefore Bob is now \(18\) years old and \(c = 6\) (\(c=6\) means Alice is \(6\) years older than Bob, which agrees with the fact that Alice is 24).
